## 1. Who We Are

LetAllGirls is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to closing the global education gap by giving underserved schools, with a focus on regions with high concentrations of girls, access to AI-powered, offline learning technology. Our mission directly addresses UN Sustainable Development Goal #4 (Quality Education) and Goal #5 (Gender Equality).

We build **D.I.D.I., the Digitally Integrated Daily Instructor**: the world's first offline, AI-enabled educational device built specifically for communities without reliable internet access.

**Mission statement (usable as-is or adapted):**
> LetAllGirls exists to make sure that a student's circumstances, whether that's where they live, their gender, or the situation they're in, never determine whether they get to learn. We build technology that works where the infrastructure doesn't. No internet, no electricity grid, no problem. So every student, regardless of their situation, has a real shot at a quality education.

## 2. The Founder's Story

In March 2023, **Ding Mayen Kuai, known to everyone as "Didi"**, was a Babson College freshman from South Sudan, unexpectedly passed away in her dorm. Didi was a social activist, unapologetically herself, and someone who had lived the exact problem LetAllGirls now exists to solve: growing up in a classroom with one overworked professor and 60 other students, no internet, no quality learning resources, and the quiet feeling of being unseen by the system meant to teach her.

Before she passed, Didi had set up a GoFundMe for an idea she called LetAllGirls. She never got to see it built. In the days after her death, more than 180 people, moved by her story, donated nearly $6,000 almost overnight.

**Mateo Acosta-Rubio**, her classmate and friend at Babson, made a decision in that moment: her vision wasn't going to end with her. He picked up what she started, and LetAllGirls became a promise, not just a nonprofit, but a way of continuing Didi's belief that every child deserves the chance to be unapologetically themselves.

With the $6,000, the team built three DIDI prototypes entirely in-house, in partnership with schools, teachers, and students in South Sudan, the first real step of Didi's vision made physical.

## 3. What D.I.D.I. Is

**D.I.D.I., Digitally Integrated Daily Instructor**

DIDI is a small, self-contained device that turns any room into a fully equipped, AI-powered classroom, without needing internet, a computer lab, or reliable electricity infrastructure to have already been built.

**Core capabilities:**
- **Works 100% offline**: no internet connection required, ever
- **Personal AI tutor**: answers student questions in real time
- **Teacher support**: helps teachers build lesson plans and track student progress
- **Digital library**: over 100+ learning resources, acting as a full library where textbooks don't exist
- **Multi-user**: supports up to 25 students connected at once
- **Region-specific**: content tailored and aligned to local national curriculums
- **Remotely updatable**: new content and improvements can be pushed to devices in the field

**How it works (3 steps):**
1. **Set up in a classroom or library.** Just press the power button.
2. **Connect any device.** DIDI broadcasts as a local WiFi network. Students and teachers connect using whatever phone, tablet, or laptop they already have.
3. **Learn.** Students explore dozens of curated resources or ask DIDI's AI a question directly.

*Note: DIDI hosts the content and AI locally. Students still need a WiFi-capable device (phone, tablet, or laptop) to connect to it.*

## 4. The Problem We're Solving

- **$160 billion** is spent on education across Africa every year, yet millions of students are still left behind.
- Only **4%** of schools in Sub-Saharan Africa have basic internet connectivity.
- Some countries have student-to-teacher ratios exceeding **60:1**.
- In some regions, there is just **one textbook for every 30 students** nationwide.

Most existing solutions assume infrastructure that doesn't exist: donated computers that need internet that was never built, curriculum redesigns with no trained teachers to deliver them. High-cost alternatives like Starlink run $600+ upfront and $200+/month, putting them out of reach for the schools that need them most.

DIDI was built as the opposite of that: cheap, offline by design, and built around the reality on the ground rather than the infrastructure we wish existed.

## 8. How We Sustain This Work

LetAllGirls operates as a nonprofit, but our device model is built for long-term sustainability, not one-time donations:

- **$200 shipping and deployment fee** per DIDI unit (often waived)
- **$25/month subscription** per device, covering remote support, software updates, and content refreshes

This model lets schools access DIDI at a fraction of the cost of alternatives like satellite internet, while giving LetAllGirls a sustainable, recurring path to fund manufacturing, R&D, and expansion instead of relying solely on grants and donations.

## 9. How to Help

A dedicated page laying out concrete ways to support LetAllGirls, beyond a generic donate button.

**1. Donate**
General donations that go toward whatever LetAllGirls needs most at the time, manufacturing, shipping, R&D, or field support.

**2. Sponsor a Device**
$25/month can support one DIDI device serving upwards of 500 students every month. This is a recurring, tangible way for an individual to see exactly what their contribution is powering.

**3. Donate Computers**
LetAllGirls partners with high schools and companies that regularly refresh their computer fleets (often every couple of years) to receive the previous generation of hardware. Some DIDI deployment sites have outdated or no end-user devices at all, so these donated computers and laptops go directly to students who would otherwise have no way to connect to DIDI.

**4. Become a Corporate Sponsor**
Companies can fund the deployment of DIDI devices, starting at $1,000+, with those devices deployed under their name at the schools they help reach. This is framed as directly funding real deployments and real students, not a generic sponsorship logo placement.

**5. Become a Partner**
For schools, school districts, and multi-school operators looking to bring DIDI into their network. LetAllGirls is actively looking for organizations that manage multiple schools to deploy DIDI at scale.
